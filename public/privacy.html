DCT Assistant data handling

This internal application sends your questions and a delegated access token to Microsoft Fabric to retrieve answers under your data permissions. In Teams, the server first exchanges your Teams token for a delegated Fabric token.

Conversation messages are held in the page's memory and cleared when the page is reloaded. Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Entra, Azure, and Fabric may process or retain service data under your organization's configuration and agreements. Clearing this page's chat does not delete those service records.

Contact your organization's application administrator for its privacy policy, retention requirements, and access requests.
